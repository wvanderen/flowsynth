Prototype assumptions and limits

Numbers are illustrative: 10 ν grant/first upgrade, 20/40 ν app ladder, 40 ν starter offers, 30 × 2ⁿ ν cells. Three starting cells. A basic generator supplies continuous adjacent charge during practice; this behavior is an assumption. Each deployed synthesizer contributes 6 ν/min at level zero, ×1.2 per level, ×1.5 when charged; an adjacent infusor adds 20% (30% when charged). Forge thresholds start at 3 charge-minutes and grow ×1.5. These are pacing probes, not proposed formula or generator decisions.

Cells grow along a fixed connected hex strip; choose module positions by stowing and placing. Free reshaping, rarity, combination, detailed focus tools and real timing are omitted. Practice advances only when you press a simulation button. Console tools never affect board production. Goal capacity is a placeholder long goal: 250 ν after two rolls.
