Opening learning arc: one synth, everything else earned

Round 1's verdict set the pedagogy: no goals, no steps, no ambient hints — the player starts with a single synth and acquires more only through play. What remains open is the one "maybe": whether a single pop-up hint about chords, delivered after the second synth is acquired, earns its place. Both variants play the identical arc — practice fills the forge; the forge offers a synth; placing it beside your first chords the pair (dashed preview while you drag, chip when it lands) — and differ only in that card.

Decide while playing: does the earned hint land at the right moment with the right words (A) — or is the dashed hull alone enough to carry "these two now chord, and the × is what it pays" (B)? Is ~2 minutes of practice the right distance to the second synth? Does the first chord feel earned when it can only arrive through play? Success test: placed, chorded, perceived, rearranged — inside minutes, with no Carrier lesson.
