Spacer module: constructing out-of-reach chords

The octave-stack board (#126) makes octaves, fifths, and fourth-shells sequential shapes — and deliberately puts third-bearing chords out of reach: a major third sits 4 lattice steps away, a minor third 3, a flat seventh only 2. Four playable spacer mechanics bridge that gap, under the interaction language of #118 (click to place, drag to move with live swap, double-click to remove) and the chord feedback of #120 (hulls, chips, dashed would-form ghosts while dragging). Same chord ladder everywhere; numbers are placeholders.

Decide while playing: which gaps a spacer bridges (fixed one-cell gap? any two-cell span? typed interval?), whether it sounds itself or only enables the chord it completes, how it places and moves, and what it should cost relative to the chords it unlocks — harder chords worth more. Height-gating is not on the table here (#122 owns it).
click empty = place palette item · drag = move (occupied drops swap) · double-click = remove
