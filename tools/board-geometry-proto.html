Prototype — throwaway, answers one question (#117)

Board geometry: what replaces the Carrier-centered distance field?

Four playable geometries, one question. Place synths, form chords, hear them, move things — in each model. Your arrangement carries across A · C · D (same hex board, different pitch laws). Sound is off until you switch it on; nothing here is tuned or final.

The bar to beat: in the opening minutes a player can place a few synths, discover or intentionally form a basic chord, perceive its effect, and rearrange the board — without understanding a Carrier, harmonic distance, or a separate chord view.
