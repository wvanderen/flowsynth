## Behavior and translation

The actual positive prestige bank now sets permanent World discovery and grants one unplaced Column plus the once-only Blade. Neither pending entitlement, generic Catalog spending, nor zero-yield prestige discovers World. Both grant receipts and discovery persist. Before discovery, World legend buttons, locked map previews and Hex-detail World faces/lines are absent. Enemy discovery, penalties, charging and combat arithmetic are unchanged.

The Arete face no longer presents the Mutator entry screen or sells Columns. Hone remains the irreversible, type-wide purchase, without an entry prerequisite. Removing the screen also removes the obsolete prerequisites on the existing capacity and Warp Beam purchases, so visible purchases remain usable. The old Column command is a side-effect-free refusal for old command/recording clients; its historical price arithmetic remains for provenance/tooling, not live acquisition.

Old saves missing the discovery field translate using evidence of prior World progression: a positive claimed frontier, the old starter receipt, or retained World collection/Hone. Pending depth, prestige count alone and generic Catalog ownership do not qualify. Eligible saves receive **one additional free Column**, even if they bought Columns historically; existing copies/positions remain, with no refund and no era reset. Retained Blades satisfy the old starter entitlement; a missing ungranted Blade is backfilled. The new grant receipt prevents replay on reload. Existing offers and unrelated progression are preserved.

Final Mutator-state/offer retirement and mixed drafts remain the other #525 slices. The recipe runner still has its legacy entry policy until that retirement; its Column purchase attempts now refuse. Its affected scenario assertion was deliberately updated: the free starter Column leaves one Arete rather than pretending it was spent. Historical recordings and frozen translation figures were not rewritten or relabeled as new-economy evidence.
