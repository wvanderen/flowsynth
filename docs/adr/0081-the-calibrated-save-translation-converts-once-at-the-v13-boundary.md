# The calibrated save translation converts once at the v13 boundary

Accepted, 2026-11-05 (issue #493, parent #484's rebalance).

## Context

The rebalance shipped its calibrated curves behind the frozen century baseline (ADR-0079), which pinned every figure the save translation would need — the claim curve, the enemy ladder, the World purchase ladders, the capacity ladder, the price curves. The baseline's raw-fixture walk recorded the stranding the translation exists to repair: a finite save written by the pre-calibration economy (runaway levels, a deep era measure, banked frontier steps priced under the retired slope) converts its un-banked depth into a frontier the calibrated curve can never re-reach — 684 Arete in the fixture's first hour, then every era open through the century mark. The parent gated this transition on the curves stabilizing and on an explicit field-by-field table; both now exist (the baseline, and `docs/save-migration.md`).

## Decision

1. **One transition, one boundary.** `SAVE_VERSION` moves to 13. Every save older than v13 passes through the calibrated translation exactly once at the deserialize boundary (`migrateV12ToV13`), after its own pinned v10 → v11 and v11 → v12 steps where present; the file the game writes next is v13, which every later load reads untouched. The version gate is the whole idempotence story.
2. **The table is the contract.** The era half takes the shipped prestige rule's own reset (levels and investment to base, nous to the opening grant, the era measure rebased, purchased capacity returned to one voice, reserves and stored combat charge emptied, every enemy restored). The retention half keeps everything the calibration never re-prices: the collection with its tiers, secondaries, board, and paid gates; the life record, practice history, goals, notes, and feats; class development; earned rolls; every purchase — Catalog unlocks, Arete offerings, the Warp Beam's rungs, scrap and the Draft Loom, the Mutator layer — and the World's purchases. The full table, grounded field by field, is `docs/save-migration.md`; the engine's migration carries the same determinations.
3. **Arete, World purchases, and the claimed frontier resolve together.** Banked Arete and World purchases stand as carried — currency and what it bought. The claimed frontier clears to zero: it is the retired curve's ledger, and held against the calibrated curve it can only strand (the raw fixture's recorded failure); cleared, it duplicates nothing, because the conversion grants no Arete and leaves no instant claim — every future claim banks against newly earned calibrated depth alone.
4. **The conversion explains itself where the save arrives.** The load that converts returns a report (figures moved, unreadable figures named); the app presents it once, at boot or import, beside the retained original bytes — which the original-save slot keeps exactly, before anything replaces them (issue #486's foundation). Canceling an import, or any failure, leaves both untouched. A nonfinite stored figure is never silently read as zero: the report names it, the working state takes its calibrated value, and the original bytes keep the history — the recovery slice's (issue #494) distinction.
5. **The baseline's schema figure re-freezes with the translation it was frozen for.** `docs/century-calibration-baseline.json`'s top-level `saveVersion` moves 12 → 13; every calibrated balance figure stays byte-identical, and the recording identity (`source.saveVersion: 12`, engine fingerprint, recording commits) keeps its historical values. The always-on century pin reads the compiled figures against the artifact, so this re-freeze is explicit, not a silent slide.

## Consequences

- Existing finite saves land playable on the calibrated economy with their earned development intact, and the collection-reset grounding's century walk is the recorded evidence the translated position sustains meaningful play. The tiered scheduled continuation (`tools/save-migration.test.ts`, `FLOWSYNTH_SAVE_MIGRATION_WALK`) banks a real claim past the translated frontier inside four credited hours on the migrated fixture.
- A modest save written after the calibration shipped (a v12 save whose economy was already calibrated) also converts: it loses its current era's levels, balances, and pending claim to the reset. The population is days wide and the explanation states exactly what moved; the alternative — conditioning the conversion on detecting "overpowered" figures — would put a second, silent economy classifier inside the boundary.
- The translation changes no field shapes: v13 bytes carry the same state schema v12 wrote, and the schema version exists to mark "this save has been translated", not to gate new fields.
- The v10 → v11 step's goal-slot nous refund remains exact within its own pinned step, but its destination is superseded for chained saves: the terminal era reset takes the whole balance onto the calibrated opening. The refund's arithmetic stays observable through the translation's cleared figures, which carry whatever the chained steps paid out.
