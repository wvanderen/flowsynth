# Start synthesizers with readable mathematical roles

Synthesizers initially contribute distinct mathematical roles, such as base additions and conditional multipliers, whose strengths depend on the build. Exponent-changing effects are deferred until simpler interactions prove interesting, keeping placement decisions understandable while preserving room for later progression complexity.

The player's production output is one final nous-per-second rate. Enter/Exit and additive synthesizers contribute to a combined base rate before production multipliers apply; modules do not make independent timed payouts. Descriptions such as 'one nous every ten seconds' express a rate of 0.1 nous per second, not a separate module payout timer.

Bonuses from copies of the same multiplier synthesizer type add into one factor; factors from distinct types multiply together. For example, two copies contributing +20% each form a ×1.4 factor, which combines with Time's ×1.2 to produce ×1.68. Local modifiers apply to each copy's contribution before those contributions are grouped.

Initial infusors improve explicitly named effects on eligible neighboring modules, and receiving charge strengthens those bonuses. They initially exclude infusor-to-infusor amplification; a module that transmits neighboring capabilities remains a future possibility.

All six activated core modules have an uncharged nous-production contribution that charge can empower: Enter/Exit supplies the base, while the others have distinct formula effects still to be designed. Their charge-generating focus interactions remain separate from these production contributions.

Charged empowerment increases with received strength with diminishing returns; the exact curve remains for balancing. When an infusor and its target are both charged, calculate the infusor's charged bonus, apply it to the target's specified production contribution, then apply the target's own charged empowerment. Infusor effects do not improve charge generation.

Both charge and infusors empower only a multiplier's bonus above ×1: a ×1.2 effect with 50% empowerment becomes ×1.3, not ×1.8. Effective bonuses from all eligible adjacent infusors add together before application to the target; for example, two +20% infusors yield +40%, and charging one to +30% makes their total +50%. The target's own charged empowerment then applies separately to its adjusted contribution.
