# Separate module upgrades from charge-earned expansion

Nous purchases permanent module upgrades, while charge earns forge rolls and cell unlocks. Purchased levels increase core power; rarity improves their scaling and secondary effects rather than granting free levels, preserving distinct roles for upgrade investment and module quality.

Combining two modules of the same type and rarity produces one of the next rarity. The output inherits the higher input level, and the lower-level input's nous upgrade expenditure is refunded so upgrading useful duplicates does not become wasted investment. The player chooses one input's secondary effects to retain with the new rarity's improvements; the two effect sets do not merge.

Combination preserves both inputs' earned charge: stored charge is summed, and queued bursts retain their original strengths and durations. The new rarity affects future generation rather than retroactively strengthening earned bursts. Other investments remain undecided; removable gems preserved through combination are a future possibility.

Initially, a forge roll offers three candidates and grants the selected module, with no consolation resources for unchosen candidates. Extra choices and improved rarity probabilities are possible later upgrades.

Habit development comes from practice time and unlocks habit-specific customization options; nous upgrades the Habit module separately. Initial goals use configurable templates defining valid practice conditions, equipped benefits, and completion-charge rules, with players choosing habits and targets; estimated-effort reward upgrades remain a future possibility.

Both live and manually logged practice count toward habit development. With no habit selected, the Habit module retains its purchased base effect but grants no habit-specific bonuses or development; unassigned session time is not retroactively reassigned for gameplay rewards.
