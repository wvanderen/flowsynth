# 0065 — Charge-response support across the charge behaviors

Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Accepted
Issue: #376 (parent #366)

## Context

The booster category held one non-amplitude primary effect — the Chorus's
chord response (ADR-0064) — while the parent's accepted sample named its
pair: the Exciter, a booster that "improv[es] charge response; no
amplitude bonus and needs charged recipients". The parent's contract
fixes the algebra on the remaining axis: a charge-response booster
strengthens the recipient's authored charge behavior — alternatives
included — without granting a second behavior or base production;
contributions to the same term add; received charge strengthens the
booster's own primary effect; and progression scales strengths, never
conditions.

## Decision

1. **The charge response is a booster primary effect.** The Exciter
   authors `exciterBonus` (0.5, provisional) and grants it to every
   adjacent oscillator, coverage as the Booster's, the Field's, and the
   Chorus's. The grant lands on the recipients' authored charge response
   — the coefficient that scales the charged curve above ×1 — and never
   on the amplitude leg or any base term. Same-term grants add: two
   Exciters beside one voice contribute ×1.0 of response between them.

2. **The grant rides the authored behavior, wherever it reads.** An
   ordinary producer's coefficient scales the empowerment delta beside
   the authored figure; the Blaster's conversion reads the effective
   response on its promoted delta (×0 uncharged preserved, the
   asymptote's share deepened, never a second empowerment pass); the
   Sideband's charge-to-chord scaling reads it too (the charge leg stays
   exactly ×1 — the conversion the charge rides is what deepens). One
   engine read per axis — the effective charge response, and the
   conversion scale over it — so the rate pass, the certified allocator,
   previews, and the breakdown rows cannot disagree.

3. **An uncharged recipient reads exactly its uncharged value.** The
   grant lands on a coefficient the charge curve's delta multiplies, and
   an uncharged voice's delta is zero: the requirement "needs charged
   recipients" is structural, so no mutator or progression can erase it —
   a power mutator scales the grant through the host power, a charge
   mutator moves the delta through the strength the recipient receives,
   and a charge-starved board reads the same rate with or without an
   Exciter.

4. **Received charge strengthens the Exciter's own primary effect.** The
   grant rides the continuous-charge curve over the Exciter's own
   received strength × its host power, exactly as every booster's grant
   does (ADR-0063's law, one axis further). Levels, tiers, and mutators
   scale the strengths — never the conditions or the proportions.

## Consequences

The rare band holds two designs (the Sideband, the Exciter), so the
roll's rare band stops sampling a single type. Charge-fed builds gain a
support module whose value is conditional on the very resource that
feeds their producers — the deepest curves (the Saw's authored ×3, the
conversion types' alternatives) profit most, while a charge-starved
board gains nothing and the amplitude and chord legs never move.
Coefficients, rarity, and the demonstration boards are provisional
tuning under the parent (#366); balance work may retune every figure
without touching this contract.
