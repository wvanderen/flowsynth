# Open the focus apps free and account sessions by trust

The focus-tool elevation (issue #68) rebuilt the session's accounting and made the whole console free. Three decisions amend standing ADRs; the full design lives in the focus-tool spec (`docs/focus-tool-spec.md`), consolidated by issue #75 from the deciding tickets (research #69; grilling #70, #71, #72, #73, #74, #76, #77), 2026-09-18/19.

## Decision

- **Every launch focus app is free from the first session** (issue #74). Habit, Time, Notes, and Goals are all active at minute 0; Time's after-first-session auto-activation milestone is struck, and session one can be planned. The loud summary's unlock row stays in design, inert until Tasks joins. Amends ADR-0012's activation provisions and the per-app launch inventory.
- **The activation ladder rests empty at launch** (issue #74). With Notes and Goals free, the ladder has no launch tenant: the catalog omits its activation section entirely until Tasks gives the ladder a tenant — no telegraph row — and ADR-0013's "rung one below the shelf floor" pricing is superseded; the ladder's shared, scaling, free-order shape stands, but its pricing is decided with that first tenant. Amends ADR-0013's ladder provisions and opening beats: the unguided rung-one-vs-generator first-spend choice dies, and the shelf generator's hinted pull is the only spend path.
- **Session time is accounted by trust, not confirmed by dialog** (issue #70). Presence is always trusted; away time on a planned session is trusted up to its target; away time past the target — and all away time on open-ended sessions, beyond a small reconciliation floor — is provisional, landing in a flagged bucket adjudicated by a mandatory, non-dismissible **honesty report** (didn't practice / did what I planned / practiced the whole time away; two options without a plan). The report's answer sets **credited practice time**, the seam every focus-side consumer keys off: habit accrual, goal progress, the charge window, and session achievements. Sleep gaps, tab discard, mid-session reload, and save-import all reconcile through these same rules. Supersedes ADR-0010's interruption reconciliation: the 120 s freeze-and-confirm-or-discard dialog and its reward gating are retired; a running session resumes on relaunch, and nothing is ever closed without the player's answer.
- **The charge window keys off credited practice time**: ADR-0012's `fraction × that session's live practice time` becomes `fraction × that session's credited practice time`. Manual logs still never bank one.

## Consequences

- **Supersedes ADR-0010's "Interruption reconciliation: pause and confirm"** — the `RECONCILIATION_THRESHOLD_SECONDS` constant, the freeze, and the confirm-or-discard dialog. ADR-0010's persistence provisions stand, now carrying the provisional bucket, honesty events, and session records as additive v5 fields.
- **Amends ADR-0012**: app activation (four apps free at the first session; the ladder rests empty), the per-app launch inventory, and the charge window's basis. The boundary rule, the console, and the category landscape stand.
- **Amends ADR-0013**: the ladder's launch contents and rung-one pricing, Time's after-first-session auto-activation (struck jointly with ADR-0012's amendment above), and the opening beats — renumbered as grant → first upgrade; session one (open-ended, steered short); loud summary with nothing firing; generator pull; generator → charge → Forge → first roll; first cell purchase; capstone console long goal. Session one's steered ~5-minute suggestion copy is reinstated for session one only; ADR-0018's flavor cut otherwise stands.
- `CONTEXT.md` is reconciled: new terms present time, away time, provisional bucket, provisional pool, honesty report, honesty event, honesty outcome, credited practice time, overrun, target-hit signals, session record, session history, miss marker, reflection, habit-keyed note, practice run, and practice calendar; Charge window, Habit, Time app, App activation, Activation ladder, Catalog, Planned target, Notes app, and Session summary are rewritten.
- **Target arrival is no longer soundless** (issues #71, #76): the first moment a planned session's wall clock reaches its target, a synthesized chime, a silent page notification, and the tab-title flip fire together, and the session enters overrun — new behavior, recorded here because the ledger's retirement row points at this ADR; the full design lives in the spec's §4–5.
- The redesign spec's drifted lines (§2.3's launch inventory, §3's ladder, §5's beats, §9's reconcile mention, §12's reflections) are annotated → ADR-0019 and the focus-tool spec.
