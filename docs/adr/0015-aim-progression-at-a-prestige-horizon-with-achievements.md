# Aim progression at a prestige horizon with achievements

Playtesting asked "the number goes up — but what's next?" Issues #13 (2026-09-14, plus its Forge-meter addendum), #26 (2026-09-15), and #27 (2026-09-15) decided the answer: a grand numeric objective on a prestige horizon, an achievement layer, and a status monitor that puts both in place of the old formula bar and grid overview.

## Decision

- **The chase is the Arete accumulator**: one full-width log-scale rail filling on **lifetime total nous earned** toward the first prestige threshold — the **horizon line**. Filling **mints Arete**: nous is the filling resource, Arete the created one. How much a fill mints, what it spends on, and what resets are prestige design, explicitly out of scope. The accumulator exposes a **reserved prestige button, inert at launch** — pressing it acknowledges the horizon (one achievement credits the gesture); its slot is real so the UI shape survives prestige's eventual design.
- **Graduations, not levels**: log-scale decade marks are visible and inert. The intermediate ladder already exists as the paced beats priced in nous (shelf, activation rungs, cells, console long goals); the bar strings them visually. A **practice-relative beat readout** rides the fill head ("next mark ≈ N of practice at this rate"), flipping anchoring near the right edge; past the horizon it yields to an "Arete minted" state. Sessions-completed and an inert Arete counter sit as secondaries below.
- **The status monitor is the horizon rail** (issue #27, variant C): a full-width rail beneath the board carrying **exactly three elements** — the live formula chip and the Forge progress meter docked as chips on its top edge (now / this stretch), the accumulator spanning beneath (the era). The hard cap guards against the dissolved grid overview regrowing. Its stats stay dispositioned: expansion meter and cell tokens retire; banked rolls move to the Forge surface; charge to board and module surfaces; counts to the views they describe. The mode-guidance line remains the console's business, not the monitor's.
- **Achievements accelerate, never gate** — the amended any% philosophy: necessary progress never requires them; difficult feats never gate the number. **Detection is live**: a static achievement registry in the engine (pure, testable), checked at action boundaries and session ticks. The v5 save stores an `id → unlockedAt` map; definitions live in code, never in the save. Unlocks toast immediately in upgrade mode; in-session unlocks queue into an "unlocked this session" row of the session summary. Nothing can unlock during session one.
- **Buff rewards ship at launch as one global achievement term**: `rate = composite × empowerment × achievementBoost` (ADR-0014), each achievement adding into the term, nous-rate only, shown as one legible line in the live rate breakdown. This supersedes the progression decision's original rewards-deferral clause (issue #13) via issue #26.
- **UI home**: a trophy glyph on the console's status strip opens the always-visible full list with progress bars; unlock toasts deep-link there. No secret achievements at launch.
- **The launch set is 17 feats** — practice capstones, console encouragers, board-and-economy encouragers, formula-and-horizon feats, and counter ladder seeds — enumerated in the redesign spec (issue #19). Names are provisional.

## Consequences

- ADR-0008's milestone-and-store evaluation provisions are superseded by the accumulator and the paced beats; the ten-minute session survives only as an onboarding tuning reference (ADR-0013).
- Terminology of record: **Arete**, **Arete accumulator**, **status monitor**, **horizon line**, **achievement** ("charge panel" was rejected as colliding with the board's Charge; "feat" is flavor individual names may carry, never a second term).
- The 100k horizon, log floor, graduation values, hue mapping, and the current engine's term mapping remain provisional tuning.
