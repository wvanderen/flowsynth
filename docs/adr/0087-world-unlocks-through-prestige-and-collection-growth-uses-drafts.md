# World unlocks through prestige and collection growth uses drafts

Accepted design; implementation pending. See the [confirmed feedback brief](../world-layer-feedback-brief.md).

World unlocks permanently on the first prestige that banks positive Arete, granting one Column alongside the once-only starter Blade. A current-era nous gate re-hides a persistent collection after reset; a Catalog entry purchase obscures the discovery behind another transaction. Enemy pressure remains before unlock but must be explained outside the hidden map.

Columns cease to be Arete purchases. Additional Columns and Blades temporarily share ordinary module drafts after unlock: a 25% chance of one World candidate, equally split between those types, retaining at least two module choices. Special drafts and existing offers are unchanged. Hone remains a type-wide Arete purchase. This trades the open-ended Column sink for collection-driven acquisition; a dedicated weapon pool is deferred until the weapon roster grows. Past Column spending is not refunded, by the sole player's explicit choice; existing collection is retained.

This supersedes ADR-0074's conflicting World entry/acquisition and experimental refund promises for this scope, and ADR-0079's premise that Columns remain the open-ended Arete sink. It does not rewrite frozen historical calibration or save-translation arithmetic, nor claim that those recordings validate this new economy. Existing unrelated reset and claim-grant contracts remain intact.
