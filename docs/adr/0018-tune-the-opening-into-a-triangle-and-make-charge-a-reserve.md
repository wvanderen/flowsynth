# Tune the opening into a triangle, shelve a synth, and make charge a reserve

The first played sessions of the polish pass (issue #46, 2026-09-16) exposed three early-game problems: the two opening cells face away from each other, so the shelf's generator cannot charge an adjacent Forge without buying a cell first; no chord is possible until a Forge roll happens to yield a synthesizer, even though the spec promises the octave chord "teachable in session one"; and the shelf's basic generator contradicted the charge vision by emitting at full strength continuously through a whole session — a 1:1 time ratio and up — instead of banking a small reserve that the next session spends.

## Decision

- **The opening board is a triangle.** The Carrier stays pinned at the origin; the two starting empty cells are chosen adjacent to each other as well, so any two of the three opening cells touch. Generator → Forge charging and Carrier-adjacent chord play are reachable with the opening footprint alone.
- **The starter shelf sells an Additive Synth.** One-time offers are now the Additive Synth, the generator, the infusor, and the Forge (all at the existing provisional prices). This amends ADR-0013's "every launch category guaranteed exactly once": the synthesizer category is guaranteed twice — the Carrier plus one harmonic — because chord play must exist before the first roll. Everything after the shelf still comes from rolls.
- **The plain generator type is retired; the focus-keyed generator is the launch generator.** Its charge-window rule is the whole generator story: no generator drips charge live. Every session end banks a charge window of 0.1 × that session's live practice time (one minute of output per ten of practice, per the original conception), spent as the generator's output during the next session's first minutes. Charge is an across-session reserve the player accrues and releases, not a constant stream. Session one therefore produces no charge anywhere; ignition is a second-session payoff. Other generators with different requirements are future work.
- **Notes are capturable between sessions as well as during flow.** A note written outside a session carries a "between sessions" marker instead of a session clock, and Marginalia keeps counting only in-flow notes.
- **Console readouts consolidate.** The activation-ladder rung telegraph is cut (the catalog's ladder rows already price every rung); production reads in one place — the console's Production slot shows the per-second rate (rates are per-second everywhere, §7) plus the live session's running total; the duplicate counter under the session clock is removed.
- **v5 saves from before this change** are remapped, not rejected: stored "generator" modules and unspent roll candidates load as focus-keyed, and shelf keys added later default to unpurchased. The version stays 5 — no players exist pre-release, and a structural rejection would erase the maintainer's review save for no gain.

## Consequences

- **Amends ADR-0013**: the shelf's contents ("Forge, one basic generator, and one infusor") and the category-once guarantee; the opening's "~2 empty cells" becomes a named triangle. ADR-0013's grant, cell-purchase, and ladder provisions stand.
- **Amends the §2.3 launch exception's framing**: what was one focus-keyed exception is now the launch generator rule (still live-practice-only; manual logs never bank).
- The redesign spec's §5 line "octave 1:2 … teachable in session one" is now actually true instead of aspirational.
- The Forge cannot charge during session one even when bought and placed — the summary teaches the reserve idea instead ("your generator banked a charge window"), and the charge window readout on the generator's face and the board's dim wiring previews make the banked state visible.
