# Fund task rewards with live practice allowance

Live flow-session time earns a shared task reward allowance, and task rewards consume it according to player-estimated size rather than using completion cooldowns or task-specific timers. All size tiers share the allowance so batch completion is supported and splitting a task into smaller tasks cannot increase the total available payout. Completion is recorded immediately even when allowance is insufficient; its reward remains pending until funded, and manual practice logs do not earn allowance.

The player does not need to mark a task as currently being worked on. Allowance belongs to the player, crosses habits, and carries over without a cap or expiry. Pending rewards are funded in completion order, with each waiting for full funding before the next proceeds.

A reward funded during a live session becomes available in the following session; a reward funded during upgrade mode becomes available in the upcoming session, without a claim button. Initially, equal allowance spent yields equal total reward across task sizes; size changes the amount consumed and paid, not the exchange rate. Customization may improve the module's overall reward rate.

Funded task rewards provide charge bursts from the Task module, with charge proportional to allowance spent, making module placement consequential and live flow necessary to realize the payoff.
