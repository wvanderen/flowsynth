# 0054 — Placement previews project the authoritative economy; formation quality splits its measured and applied reads

Date: 2026-10-07
Status: Accepted (development slice — the capacity economy stays behind the `?dev` gate pending calibration)
Issue: #260

## Context

ADR-0052 made the authoritative allocation the live display and production seam at capacity one, and #259 sold the ladder. Issue #260 asks the placement gesture itself to answer the capacity question: while a module moves, players see whether the proposed placement improves production — resulting final ν/s, total chord factor, formation quality, and used/available capacity — and the selected voice's readout retains the information after the drop. Two seams were missing: a projection that cannot drift from the commit, and an honest split between the quality a formation *measures* and the term an unallocated voice *applies* (#260's first two acceptance criteria).

## Decision

1. **The projection is one engine seam over the commit's own board.** `projectPlacement` (economy.ts) builds the hypothetical board exactly as `placeModule` and `returnModule` write it — the mover at its target, an occupant swapped out, a null target reading the module off the board (the retrieval) — factored out of the would-form preview (`hypotheticalBoardFor`) so recognition ghosts and rate projections can never diverge on placement semantics. The commit's boundary beats then fold onto **copied** ledgers before the display pass: the allocation retention hint (`syncRates` writes the hypothetical's `activeChords`), the chord-discovery sync, and the feats — so the previewed figures include the discovery and achievement legs the commit's render will show. The gate matches the display's own (ADR-0052): development boards project through the allocator, ordinary boards through the uncapped recognizer. The `current` snapshot rides the identical basis, so every delta the preview reads is the one the commit delivers; the suites pin snapshot equality across move, swap, tray placement, and retrieval, in both economies.

2. **Measured and applied formation quality are two reads.** The allocator's `formationQ` map is now the **applied** production term — exactly 1 on a voice carrying no active chord, whatever its formation measured — while the formation's **measured** quality rides a new `formationMeasuredQ`, scored over every singing voice in the connected formation (voices whose recognized chords sat inactive included) whenever the formation recognizes a chord at all, active or idle. `Contribution.formationQ` carries the applied term and `formationMeasuredQ` the measurement, so the rate details' Formation/chord legs multiply out exactly on an unallocated voice instead of presenting a phantom ×1/1.12 split. A formation whose every chord sits idle measures its Q and applies ×1 to everyone; a chordless formation is exactly neutral in both reads.

3. **The allocation Q curve carries its own magnitudes.** BALANCE gains `allocationComplexityRate` (0.12), `allocationTensionAllowance` (0.8), and `allocationTensionWeights` (the production shape) inside the adopted 0.5–1.5 bounds from #257 — retuned so organized formations meaningfully spread the range rather than merely wearing a lifted cap: octaves ×1.00, fifths ×1.12, triads ×1.24, organized sevenths ×1.36, lush organized clusters toward ×1.48, chromatic density at the ×0.5 floor. ADR-0049's three-way balance holds under capacity-one economics (dominant and major sevenths each outproduce a clean triad per voice; the bridged chromatic mass loses), pinned in the suite. An upper bound of two is computed beside the shipped curve in `placement-preview.test.ts` as the recorded experimental comparison — every representative formation reads identically, because the magnitudes shape the posture, not the cap — and the shipped default stays 1.5.

4. **The gesture owns the reserved readout.** While a drop hovers a valid target — a cell the register would take, or the tray under a carried module (the retrieval preview) — the readout carries the projection: a `Placement ±X ν/s` lead chip (the board-rate delta), then the moved voice's resulting rows in exactly the selected row's grammar (final ν/s, capacity, the prominent total factor, the applied Formation term, the quality scale, active and idle chord chips), each with its current figure in the tooltip. The row is transient by construction — it renders only while the hover lives — and the placed voice's selected row after the landing carries the same chips the preview showed. The **quality scale** is the low-to-high read: floor and cap end ticks with a taller neutral tick, a firm inset marker where the formation measures — position and weight carry the read, so it survives greyscale; nothing animates.

5. **Ghosts classify per instance by the projected allocation.** A would-form promise counts as earning only when the projection activates one of the instances the drop adds; a retained instance (the class merely doubling) or an unaffordable one draws in the idle candidates' dotted quiet register with "· idle" on its chip — a full budget never promises earnings it cannot afford. Capacity-driven replacements leave no stale seams: the displaced chord demotes to its idle mark, the readout names it idle exactly once, and an unrelated selection now survives another module's placement (only the placed module's own selection drops, preserving the closed-face rule).

## Consequences

- Preview cost rides the same bounded solves the dev display already runs per pass (two allocation solves per hover change in development, plain rate passes in ordinary play), cached per hover target and cleared on every render.
- The plain recognizer's snapshots leave `formationMeasuredQ` unset, defaulting to `formationQ` — ordinary play's uncapped pass applies its Q to every member of a named formation, so its measurement and application coincide (ADR-0049 unchanged).
- Ordinary boards also gain the preview panel — through the uncapped recognizer, without capacity rows — since the projection's gate is the display's own.
- The strum, the ghost geometry, and the tray gesture are untouched; only the hover refresh and the readout grew, never a re-render.
