# Share forge progress and scale roll cost globally

All deployed Forges contribute to one player-wide forge-progress meter according to received charge and their progress efficiency. Each threshold crossing banks a roll, carries excess progress forward, and increases the global next-roll threshold; this controls progression without fresh Forges resetting costs or competing local meters having their targets raised by another Forge's payout.

This supersedes local Forge progress and threshold-history inheritance. Combining, replacing, or removing Forges leaves shared progress and earned-roll history intact; upgrades and rarity change future contribution rates. Forging and expansion have separate global meters.

All rolls use the same odds in the first playable. How contributing Forges affect future roll-quality bonuses is explicitly deferred with secondary effects, rather than assigning a shared roll to one Forge or allowing a briefly equipped module to improve the whole reward.
