# The claim grant funds each attempt's rebuild, and the claim slope asks most of an era's time beyond its previous bank

Accepted balance calibration, 2026-11-07 (issue #484's later-era repair; #491's standing effort criterion).

## Context

#491's recorded later-era evaluation carried an unmet merge blocker across three re-records: across 119 closed later eras, zero spent most of their credited time beyond the previous bank's depth (the new-territory majority read), and 107 of 119 spent more than half their era regaining the production the reset erased. The century calibration (ADR-0079) had already priced the alternative knob honestly: a steeper depth demand (the slope-2 hypothesis) lengthened the push but grew bankless tails to 39 credited hours inside the horizon, because a mature board's production compounds roughly ×3–4 per productive era and cannot chase ×10-per-bank demand. The trade was recorded for a scope decision no one had made.

The re-recorded trajectories show where a later era's credited time actually went at slope 4: roughly two-thirds re-crawling the level ladder from a 12ν opening grant back to the previous era's peak production (the rebuild), the next fifth re-earning the previous bank's era-earned depth at that peak (the familiar re-walk), and only the final quarter earning the ×3.16 of depth the next claim demanded. The push can never out-weigh the rest while every reset returns the board to the opening grant — retained World strength (the claimed frontier, the Columns, the Hone ranks, the collection) shaped what the era could rebuild toward but not how fast it could start rebuilding.

## Decision

1. **The claim grant exists** (`BALANCE.prestigeGrantBase` 600000, `BALANCE.prestigeGrantGrowth` 2.4): the ν a banked claim pays beside its Arete, authored as base × growth^claimed-frontier. The reset that banks a positive claim funds its next attempt's rebuild on the frontier that claim opened; the era then starts its rebuild at roughly the previous bank's production instead of re-crawling the ladder from the opening grant. The growth tracks the rebuild investment's own compounding (the claim curve's per-bank depth against the board's per-era production growth), so the grant stays at the rebuild-to-peak scale at every reachable frontier instead of growing into a second economy.
2. **An unadvanced frontier reads the plain opening grant.** A fresh game and every zero-claim reset pay exactly what they always paid (12ν, or the recipe's declared figure): the honest zero-claim reset keeps its shipped meaning — story 28 — and mints nothing its claim did not bank. The grant lands once, at the reset that banked, and reads the claimed frontier, which only advances on claims.
3. **The grant's one payer is the prestige action, and its arithmetic refuses whole.** `eraStartGrantOf` is the one read (the reset that pays it, the playthrough ledger that counts it); a frontier deep enough to price the grant past the ν representation overflows to Infinity and the reset refuses whole before banking anything (issue #486's precedent) — never a banked claim beside an unrepresentable balance.
4. **The claim slope re-prices at 2.5** (`BALANCE.world.claimDepthSlope`, from ADR-0079's 4): each banked two-Arete claim now demands ×6.3 of era-earned depth, so the push beside a banked claim carries most of an era's credited time. The slope-4 century fix had matched demand to production growth while leaving every era's effort below its previous bank's depth; with the grant compressing the rebuild, the steeper demand serves the effort criterion without re-creating the slope-2 bankless tails — the recorded century walks still bank through the 100-credited-hour mark (6–7 productive banks per century on the recorded walks).
5. **The frozen baseline re-freezes under the same v13 boundary** (ADR-0079's standing rule, exercised for real): `docs/century-calibration-baseline.json` gains the claim-grant figures and the re-priced slope, compiled from the shipped constants and guarded by `tools/century.test.ts`. The save translation's conversion table is untouched — a converted save lands at a cleared frontier, so its era-start grant reads the plain opening grant, and every later claim banks against the re-priced curve through the same one-time boundary (ADR-0081).

## Consequences

- **The effort criterion's read moves onto the recorded matrix.** The recorded later-era distribution at the calibrated pair (grant + slope) holds most closed later eras above the new-territory majority and almost none above the rebuilding majority, with eras straddling the 5–10 credited-hour window and every miss explicit. The evidence lives in the re-recorded evaluation and its report figures; this ADR claims the direction, not the row counts.
- **The opening era is untouched.** Era zero starts at frontier zero, reads the plain opening grant, and banks at the entry depth whatever the slope reads — #490's 8–12-hour calibration stands unchanged, and the recorded first-prestige recheck re-records beside it.
- **A century walk reaches a shallower frontier count at the steeper demand** — each bank is deeper, so the same depth budget divides into fewer banks (frontier ~14 versus ~25 on the recorded slope-4 walks). The World roster's reachable span within the horizon narrows accordingly; the roster beyond the horizon remains authored content, as ADR-0079 already recorded for its upper decades.
- **The walk ledger counts the grants.** Era-start grants bypass `totalEarned` (they are authored intake, never earned production), so the playthrough record carries `grantNous` beside the sessions' and breathers' figures, and the no-grant integrity reads stay between earned production and recorded production.
- **The playtesting override keeps its shape.** The declared `openingGrant` tuning figure replaces the grant whole for an isolated run — a declared flat value, never the curve — so #472's isolation story holds verbatim.
