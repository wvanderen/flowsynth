# Resolve the roster: silent voices, a charge conduit, and a charge-sourced voice

The decision map (issue #212, ticket #215, 2026-10-02) turned the richer-roster candidate list parked in issue #177 into a coherent roster for the next iteration: which candidate types exist, what each role does, and what each does not. The foundation was pitch ownership: ADR-0021's cell-owned pitch stands unamended, so every pitch modifier is a voice sounding a derived pitch on its own cell — no module ever rewrites another cell's pitch, and columns keep reading as one note name.

## Decision

- **A new silent-voice category** (working name): silent pitched modules that produce no nous but count in chord clusters — they form and complete chords and conduct them as voices. The category trait is a level-scaled uplift to every chord instance's bonus that a silent voice sings in, landing on all singing members and stacking additively across silent voices. Three types, differentiated only by pitch source: the **Harmonizer** sings its own cell's pitch; the **Mirror voice** sings an adjacent voice's pitch one octave down — a guaranteed Octave pairing that doubles the neighbor's chord content without touching its pitch or readout (the Sub Bass direction, which under register-free chords is void at its own cell's class); the **Shift voice** sings its own pitch altered by a player-picked small interval — the ♯/♭ family, ±1 at launch — with the selectable shift set growing with rarity while level scales its uplift (the Accidental direction, FM folded in rather than a separate module).
- **The Conditional transforms in place into the Harmonizer.** Save migration with a version bump; the per-instance chordAmp-on-own-production mechanic dies — it was the roster's muddiest effect, and keeping two chord-support mechanics would have forked the harmony work (issues #217, #218) across both.
- **The Amplifier** founds a charge-conduit category (working name): it receives charge from generators or other amplifiers and re-broadcasts to its other neighbors at received strength × a level-scaled gain. Relayed charge counts fully as receiving charge everywhere — empowerment, Forge thresholds, RITUAL, build effects. Cycle safety is a hop-depth cap (provisional 4). It is not a generator and produces nothing; since generator reserves burn 1 s/s regardless of receivers, the gain is the real balance lever.
- **The Blaster** is the synthesizer category's second producer role, converting received charge into its synth term on the existing synths leg — the rate equation is unchanged. Its chord membership is structural: it sings and completes chords even uncharged at zero output. Its charge-sourced output takes chord factors and infusor uplift, and takes no second charged-empowerment pass — the conversion curve replaces the charge factor rather than stacking on it. This restores ADR-0004's distinct-mathematical-roles intent with two producer roles: the Additive's base term and the Blaster's charge-sourced term. Generators never divide output among neighbors, so a Blaster adds a receiver without starving cellmates.
- **Renames.** Synthesizer → Oscillator is adopted in direction; Infusor → Booster is rename-only, the uplift role untouched; the Additive keeps its name this iteration, the rename handed over with a no-collision constraint — "Note" collides with the Notes app, the note generator, and the Note pool, "Voice" with chord voices. Final names, glyphs, hues, and category names belong to the roster prototype (issue #219).
- **Acquisition is module-roll pool only for every new type** — consistent with RITUAL's pool-only arrival (ADR-0046) and the consideration of scaling back one-time guaranteed offerings; no new guarantees are added, and the starter shelf's own fate is the release-scoping ticket's (issue #221).
- **Habit builds stay two-branch with no new nodes.** The roster supplies targets — charge-side effects reach Amplifiers, Blasters, and RITUAL through the existing legs — and the harmony tickets supply the chord levers. The Focus/Note/Goal generator family stands as designed (ADR-0047); only their final names ride with #219.

## Consequences

- Supersedes ADR-0036's clause keeping the Conditional's separate per-instance bonus (ADR-0022) on top of the chord factor — that mechanic, and the Conditional type with it, is retired by the transformation.
- The chord matcher's voices become synthesizers and silent voices together; spacers still conduct without singing. Hulls, seams, and the reserved readout need one honest convention for marking silent voices as muted participants.
- ADR-0021 and ADR-0036 are upheld, not superseded: pitch stays in the cell and chord bonuses stay local — the Blaster's charge-sourced term rides the synths leg with its own local chord factor.
- The chord vocabulary for shifted and mirrored content — which chromatic and octave-family chords get named — belongs to the chord discovery library (issue #218); all magnitudes, hop caps, shift tables, and conversion curves are provisional tuning per the map's standing note.
