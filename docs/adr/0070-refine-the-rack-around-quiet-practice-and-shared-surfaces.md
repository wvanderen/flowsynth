# Refine the Rack around quiet practice and shared surfaces

The visual-cohesion interview on 2026-10-17 preserves the Rack identity and main layout while allowing supporting controls to be regrouped and aligned. Module faces remain the visual center; the surrounding substrate, panel tiers, rules, muted text, and scrim move together to neutral graphite so category and resource colors carry the emphasis. This amends ADR-0016's indigo substrate choice, retaining its semantic color mappings.

Clipping identifies floating outer instrument panels only: top-right and bottom-left, with square controls and internal divisions. Phone sheets follow that orientation rather than the Catalog's sloping whole top edge. These explicit rules replace the previous discretionary “occasional” clipping guidance without moving the historical prototype pins.

During flow, activity should become apparent on deliberate inspection rather than repeatedly attracting peripheral attention. Use steady low illumination and subtle local modulation instead of broad background breathing and strong repeated flashes; this amends ADR-0016's threshold-flash prescription. Meaningful events, such as goal or session completion and a newly formed chord, may receive a brief local accent; continuous charging remains subdued. Reduced-motion presentation is fully static, communicating charge and direction through persistent marks and illumination instead of retaining moving leads.

The visual library serves both whole-instrument design comparison and isolated component development. It must render production-backed controls, panels, module states, populated boards, disclosures, and motion samples; its hosting tool remains an implementation choice after proving that coverage. Existing prototypes remain historical approval evidence, not a second implementation of the library.

Preserve existing choices and behavior in this round, including Focus duration presets. Improve grouping, spacing, tabs, and emphasis without simplifying the interaction model. A future discovery iteration covers practice-setup simplification and the Habits sheet within the Focus modal, tracked in [#407](https://github.com/wvanderen/flowsynth/issues/407).

This records accepted direction, not completed implementation or visual validation. Exact palette values and detailed component coverage are implementation/prototype work within this direction; standards outside these explicit amendments remain in force.
