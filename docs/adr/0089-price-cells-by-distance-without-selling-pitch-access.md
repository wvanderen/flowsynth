# Price cells by distance without selling pitch access

Accepted design; not implemented. Modules and World share one footprint. Each connected expansion cell costs nous according to its hex distance from a fixed origin. Cells in the same band have the same price, regardless of purchase order. Keep the starter triangle, make a few nearby additions attainable, and price farther bands steeply. Octaves still determine pitch, not permission to build.

Farther space must require buying farther space: free reshaping can rearrange owned cells only without increasing ownership in any distance band. Moving a module or World occupant between owned cells remains free. Retaining unrestricted reshaping would let cheap inner cells buy expensive outer reach; removing reshaping entirely would take away inexpensive layout experiments.

This supersedes the expansion-pricing and gate choice in ADR-0022, not the musical lattice. Tune against a working opening module setup and a viable first World approach; reaching an enemy must not demand an unrelated unlock or filling a whole ring. See the [redesign brief](../world-combat-redesign-brief.md) for proof scope.
