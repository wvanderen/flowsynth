# The rate details are module-linked

Playing with the local-chord board (ADR-0036) exposed the next honesty gap (issue #154, 2026-09-27): the Rate cell led with an operand chain — `(synths + infusors) × emp × ach` — whose legs aggregate the whole board, so nothing on the surface answered the player's actual question, "which module makes this, and what changed it?" The session summary and the reserved readout had per-module figures; the rate disclosure itself did not. This ADR records the shape that landed.

**Amended 2026-10-14 (issue #334, the 2026-10-13 play-feedback interview): the roster answers what the board earns and nothing else.** The supporting-module section is retired outright — the infusor, generator, Forge, and the rest disclose in the local calculations and their own module surfaces, never as roster rows. The per-row tooltip legs die with the Achievements/Discoveries duplication: the global Feats and Discoveries factors show once, on their own line, worded as already included, and each oscillator row leads with its final ν/s as before. The row's deeper mechanics move from the tooltip layer to an explicit inline calculation — one oscillator at a time, opened and closed by the row's own control, keyboard and touch alike (the ADR-0056 interview's confirmed exception to tooltip-only mechanics disclosure) — and the legs multiply back to the row's figure exactly, the global leg closing the chain. The instrument standards' "deeper mechanics live in the tooltip layer, never expandable Details sections" clause is excepted here by the same interview: the calculation is an explicit control's reveal, not a `<details>` default, and the ledger's click/tap rate sheet is its reliable door.

## Decision

- **The Rate cell shows the final total, and the total is the display at every width.** The ambient operand chain dies — there is no width where the collapsed equation stands instead of the figure — and with it the leg-level tooltip prose riding its terms.
- **The disclosure is one module-linked roster, shared by every channel.** Above the 760px breakpoint the Rate cell's hover or focus opens the popover for the quick read; a tap — the Rate cell's at every width, the phone game-info strip's read's on phone — opens the same roster as a sheet over a scrim, so a touch surface above the line (no hover, no focus-on-tap) still has a door. One builder renders both, both through live slots the tick fills.
- **One row per synthesizer, leading with its final ν/s.** *Amended by issue #334: the legs no longer ride a tooltip — the row's calculation control opens them inline, one oscillator at a time, the global Feats · Discoveries leg closing the chain so the legs multiply back to the row's figure exactly.* The row expands into the legs that built it — the base term, the local chord multiplier with its named terms ("Fifth ×1.3"; "none" when chordless), the local infusor uplift, the charge factor with the strength received, and the achievement boost — and the legs multiply back to the row's figure exactly. The roster's rows sum to the board's rate within rounding, so the details can never disagree with the total above them.
- **A synthesizer row's tap identifies its module on the board.** Selection is the board's one emphasis (ADR-0025): the row wears a picked mark, the module's face lifts into the bloom, its chords stay focused. On the sheet the tap closes the sheet first, so the answer lands on the board it names. A click inside the expanded legs is reading, not picking — it never selects a module or closes the sheet.
- **Nonproducing modules disclose effects, never ν/s.** *Retired by issue #334: the supporting-module section is gone entirely — supporting effects appear in the local oscillator calculations and their own module surfaces, so the rate details answer only what the board earns.*
- **The desktop Session read joins the tight-surface rule (amends ADR-0031's carve-out).** It formats with `formatFixed` — trailing zeros kept — and its cell reserves a lane, so the ticking session no longer breathes; the ledger was the last `formatNumber` read that moved every tick.
- **The achievements page points at the rows.** *Amended by issue #334: the page points at the rate details' one Feats factor — there are no per-row Achievements legs anymore.*

## Consequences

- Supersedes ADR-0030's "the operand chain never goes ambient on phone" clause and the spec's ambient-chain wording: there is no chain anywhere to keep non-ambient. ADR-0030's other provision — one feats surface, the strip's read as the phone's door — stands, with the door now opening the module-linked sheet.
- Amends ADR-0031: "ledgers keep `formatNumber`" loses the Session cell, which now pays the fixed-width cost like the other tight surfaces.
- The empowerment leg keeps no surface of its own; it survives in the engine's rate identity (`rate = (synths + infusors) × empowerment × achievementBoost`) and the session summary's legs. A reader who wants the aggregate can still add the rows.
- The sheet's rows are expandable `<details>` whose figures ride the same live slots the popover fills: the tick updates them in place, and only a roster or feats change rebuilds the sheet — an expanded row survives the clock, and a tick never collapses it. *Amended by issue #334: never a `<details>` element — the calculation is the row's own control's reveal, and the same live-slot rule holds: a tick fills the open calculation in place and never collapses it.*
