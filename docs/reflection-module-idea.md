# Reflection module — future idea

Captured during the design interview on 2026-09-12; not part of the first playable or an accepted addition to the required core modules.

A later-game, possibly optional focus module could offer a couple of reflection prompts after a focus session completes, including a self-reported focus assessment, and produce charge when those prompts are filled in. Charge rewards logging the reflection, not the reported score; an honest low rating must not reduce the reward.

Optional versus required deployment, prompt content, assessment scale, reward timing, and reward limits remain open. This note records the idea without deciding those details.
